Through educational sessions, mentorship, community activities, technology programs, leadership development, and opportunity awareness, FSE aims to help students take meaningful steps toward their futures.

THE NEED

Why Future Scholars Ethiopia?

Ethiopia has made important progress in expanding access to education, but access to school does not always mean access to quality learning, reliable information, mentorship, technology, or future opportunities.

UNICEF reports that only 33.1% of children were reaching secondary school in 2021/22, while approximately 90% of 10-year-olds were unable to read or understand a simple text-based sentence.

The World Bank and UNESCO's 2024 assessment similarly estimated that 89% of Ethiopian children were not achieving minimum reading proficiency at the end of primary school.

Students also face a growing digital and opportunity gap. UNESCO reported in 2025 that 79% of Ethiopians remained unconnected to the Internet, limiting access to online learning, research, scholarships, universities, and global opportunities.

THE CHALLENGE Barriers Students Face
  • 1 Limited access to reliable educational information
  • 2 Lack of mentorship and guidance
  • 3 Difficulty understanding university and scholarship pathways
  • 4 Unequal access to technology and learning resources
  • 5 Limited exposure to global opportunities
  • 6 Financial and geographic barriers
  • 7 Limited opportunities to develop leadership and practical skills
  • 8 Limited exposure to STEM, research, AI, and innovation
WHERE FSE COMES IN

Making opportunity more accessible.

FSE cannot solve all of Ethiopia's education challenges. But we can contribute to a specific and practical part of the problem: making information, mentorship, skills, peer learning, and opportunity awareness more accessible to students.

OUR STRUCTURE

Seven Departments. One Purpose.

FSE's Departments are designed around one central objective: Helping students become informed, skilled, confident, and opportunity-ready.

01
🎓

Scholarship & University Mentorship Department

Purpose: Help Ethiopian students understand higher-education pathways and scholarship opportunities.

Activities: Scholarship awareness, University guidance, Application preparation, Financial-aid awareness, Personal statement guidance, Application strategy, Academic planning, Peer mentorship

Goal: To help students make informed decisions about their educational futures.

02
💪

Motivation & Leadership Development Department

Purpose: Develop students who are confident, disciplined, responsible, and capable of leading.

Activities: Leadership sessions, Personal development, Goal setting, Communication, Confidence building, Time management, Teamwork, Motivation sessions

Goal: To develop young people who can create positive change in their communities.

03
💻

STEM & Future Technology Department

Purpose: Introduce students to science, technology, engineering, mathematics, and emerging technologies.

Topics: Programming, Artificial intelligence, Data science, Scientific research, Computer science, Mathematics, Technology, Emerging technologies

Goal: To inspire students to explore the technologies shaping the future.

04
🌍

Global Skills & Diplomacy

Purpose: Prepare students for international academic, professional, and collaborative environments.

Activities: Communication, Public speaking, Cross-cultural understanding, Diplomacy, Global citizenship, International opportunities, Collaboration

Goal: To help Ethiopian students confidently engage with the global community.

05
📈

Business & Innovation Department

Purpose: Develop entrepreneurial thinking and encourage students to create solutions to real-world problems.

Activities: Entrepreneurship, Innovation, Problem-solving, Business fundamentals, Idea development, Project development, Financial awareness

Goal: To encourage students to become creators and problem-solvers.

06
🤝

Community Outreach & Ambassadors Department

Purpose: Expand FSE's educational reach and connect students across Ethiopia.

Activities: Student outreach, Ambassador programs, School engagement, Community education, Opportunity awareness, Student networking

Goal: To make educational information available to more students.

07
🪐

Astronomy Department

Purpose: Inspire curiosity about the universe and introduce students to astronomy and space science.

Activities: Astronomy education, Space science, Scientific observation, Astronomy discussions, Guest sessions, Scientific curiosity

Goal: To encourage students to explore science beyond the classroom.

ISMAEL ADINEW PRESIDENT & FOUNDER
“

A Message From the President

Education has the power to transform an individual's future and, ultimately, the future of a community. Yet many talented Ethiopian students have ambitious dreams without having access to the information, mentorship, resources, and opportunities they need to pursue them.

Future Scholars Ethiopia was created to help close that gap.

We believe students should not simply be told that opportunities exist. They should be given the knowledge and guidance to understand those opportunities and the confidence to pursue them. FSE is built around a culture of learning, collaboration, leadership, and service.

I invite students, mentors, educators, organizations, and partners to join us in building a stronger educational future for Ethiopia.

Ismael Adinew President, Future Scholars Ethiopia
01

Ismael Adinew

President & Founder, Future Scholars Ethiopia

Ismael is a student leader, education advocate, and aspiring researcher with interdisciplinary interests spanning neuroscience, artificial intelligence, data science, STEM, technology, education, and youth development.

His journey in educational leadership began with initiatives such as Study Abroad Support and High School Science Hub, which he created to help students access educational resources, discover academic and scholarship opportunities, and develop their scientific knowledge.

Driven by his growing interest in the brain and emerging technologies, Ismael also created Neuroscience Ethiopia, a student-led initiative focused on introducing young people to neuroscience and encouraging interest in brain science, research, and interdisciplinary learning.

Building on these experiences, Ismael founded Future Scholars Ethiopia (FSE) to expand his educational work and create a broader platform for Ethiopian students. FSE focuses on scholarship awareness, mentorship, STEM education, skills development, and access to academic opportunities.

Through his initiatives, Ismael aims to create a culture where students are encouraged not only to pursue opportunities for themselves, but also to share knowledge, support one another, conduct research, and create opportunities for their communities.

His academic interests lie particularly at the intersection of neuroscience, data science, and artificial intelligence. He is interested in using computational and data-driven approaches to investigate complex questions about the brain and human learning.
